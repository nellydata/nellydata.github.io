03 / About the studio

Thoughtful work.
Room to explore.

NellyData is an independent, student-led studio focused on games, interactive systems, and the ideas that connect them.

The work draws on a background in technology and data analysis, alongside experience in operations and ongoing game development studies. The goal is to make thoughtful projects and be honest about where they are in development.
